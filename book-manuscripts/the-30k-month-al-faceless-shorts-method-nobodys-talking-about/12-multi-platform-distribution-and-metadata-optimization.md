The core operational rule of multi-platform syndication is The Native Optimization Protocol. You must never cross-post a video from TikTok to YouTube Shorts with a visible platform watermark. Always export clean, uncompressed master MP4 files from your editing timeline, publishing the raw, un-watermarked asset natively to each platform.
