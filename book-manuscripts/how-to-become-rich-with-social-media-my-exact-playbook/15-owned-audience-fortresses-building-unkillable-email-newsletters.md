Maintain immaculate Email Deliverability Hygiene. Deliverability is the technical science of ensuring that your emails land directly in the primary inbox rather than being routed to the spam or promotions tab. Authenticate your sending domain with proper SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records. Never purchase scraped email lists; only email subscribers who have opted in directly. Regularly prune dormant subscribers who have not opened an email in ninety days to maintain open rates above thirty-five percent. Pristine deliverability hygiene with authenticated DNS records and regular subscriber pruning ensures emails reach primary inboxes.
