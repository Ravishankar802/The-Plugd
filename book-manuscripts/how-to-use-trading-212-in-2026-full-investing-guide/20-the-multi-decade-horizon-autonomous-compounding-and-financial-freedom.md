In institutional endowment management, the standard baseline is William Bengen's famous Four Percent Rule, developed through extensive historical back-testing of thirty-year retirement periods. The four percent rule posits that an investor can withdraw four percent of their initial portfolio value upon retirement, adjusting that withdrawal amount annually for inflation, with near-zero probability of exhausting capital. Implementing safe decumulation rates protects retirement portfolios from premature exhaustion across varying macroeconomic environments.
