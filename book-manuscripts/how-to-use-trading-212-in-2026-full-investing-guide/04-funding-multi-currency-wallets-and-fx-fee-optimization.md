Always maintain a strict policy regarding Base Currency Selection. When you initially register your Trading 212 account, you must select your primary base currency, which should invariably be the legal tender of your primary residence and income source. Beware of Currency Risk in International Portfolios. When you invest in foreign assets denominated in US Dollars or Japanese Yen, your total return is the combined product of the asset's share price performance and the fluctuation in exchange rates between your home currency and the foreign currency. If an American stock rises by ten percent but the US Dollar depreciates by eight percent against your domestic currency, your net return upon conversion is merely two percent. While currency fluctuations tend to balance out over multi-decade horizons, an investor must remain aware of this macroeconomic dynamic and structure their multi-currency reserves with deliberate foresight.
